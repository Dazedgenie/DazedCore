description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Heavy parts:[/b] anything over 30 kg (big tanks, solar arrays, controllers) is carried as parts, (1/2) and (2/2), just like a vanilla bed or shelving. Placing needs every part on you.
description=[*][b]Building Picker:[/b] click houses, basements or structures you built yourself to serve them with power or water. Green is served, yellow adds, blue removes, red is out of reach.
description=[*][b]One sandbox preset:[/b] Custom, Easy, Standard, Realistic or Hardcore on the Dazed Core sandbox page sets the options of Power, Plumbing, Climate and Essentials at once.
description=[*][b]Dazed Mod Suite Guide:[/b] a book button at the foot of the left sidebar opens an in-game guide, with tabs for Basics, Power, Plumbing, Butchery, Cooking and Dank when those mods are on.
description=[*][b]Shared options page:[/b] every Dazed tick box and key lives under one "Dazed Core" entry in the Mods options tab.
description=[*][b]Temperatures:[/b] one setting (game setting, Celsius or Fahrenheit) that Climate, Dank, the EVs and the thermometer all follow.
description=[*][b]Plays nice:[/b] a Dazed feature steps aside when another mod you run already does the same job.
description=[*][b]Debug spawn:[/b] in debug mode or for server staff, a "Dazed debug" right-click menu spawns a mod's whole item set for testing.
description=[*][b]Server sync:[/b] the server is the authority, clients stay in step, and a line above your head tells you why something was refused.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]The Dazed suite[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Core/images/suite.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Dazed Power[/b]: solar, wind, pedal, water, steam, gas and wood gas power feeding battery banks, with a charge controller and wired buildings
description=[*][b]Dazed Power: Electric Vehicles[/b]: homemade 1993 electric vehicles built from car batteries and charged from your Power system
description=[*][b]Dazed Plumbing[/b]: tanks, pipes, pumps, wells, rain collection, purifiers and a water main that brings a whole house back to life
description=[*][b]Dazed Dank[/b]: realistic cannabis growing: strains, grow rooms, hydro, drying, curing and smoking
description=[*][b]Dazed Dank: Garden Crops[/b]: grow vanilla crops in Dank's grow bags and hydro
description=[*][b]Dazed Climate[/b]: a temperature overhaul: real winters, rooms that hold heat, frost on crops, row tunnels and freezing pipes
description=[*][b]Dazed Butchery[/b]: pick a primal from the carcass and trace every cut yourself; hidden grades, field dressing, dry-aging
description=[*][b]Dazed Cooking[/b]: every cut has a best method and a best doneness; cook it right and your survivor feels it. Drying racks and a food dehydrator too
description=[*][b]Dazed Essentials[/b]: common-sense quality of life, every feature a sandbox switch
description=[/list]

description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Core/images/h-authors.png[/img]
description=Dazed Core is a library other mods can build on: a power registry (register a provider or a load and ask [i]isPowered(obj)[/i] about any object), building and reach resolution, synced ModData tables, a client-to-authority request layer with rate limits, mod detection so features can yield to another mod, schema migration, one temperature lookup for every mod, 20 shared part tags (motors, compressors, copper tubing, wiring harnesses and more) that any Dazed recipe can name, a debug spawn menu mods add item sets to, and a shared Error Magnifier report. Full API notes and headless tests are in the GitHub repo.
