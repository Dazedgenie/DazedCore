description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Heavy parts[/b] – anything over 30 kg (big tanks, solar arrays, controllers) is carried as parts, (1/2) and (2/2), just like a vanilla bed or shelving. Placing needs every part on you.
description=[*][b]Building Picker[/b] – click houses, basements or structures you built yourself to serve them with power or water. Green is served, yellow adds, blue removes, red is out of reach. Made to work on dedicated servers too.
description=[*][b]One sandbox preset[/b] – Custom, Easy, Standard, Realistic or Hardcore sets every Dazed mod's options at once from a single Dazed Core page in the sandbox options.
description=[*][b]Dazed Mod Suite Guide[/b] – a book button at the foot of the left sidebar opens an in-game guide with a tab for each Dazed mod you have enabled.
description=[*][b]Shared options page[/b] – all Dazed tick boxes live under one “Dazed Core” entry in the Mods options tab.
description=[*][b]Server sync[/b] – the server is the authority, clients stay in step, and a line above your head tells you why something was refused.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]The Dazed suite[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Core/images/suite.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Dazed Power[/b] – solar, wind, steam and gas generators feeding battery banks, with a controller monitor and cabled buildings
description=[*][b]Dazed Power: Electric Vehicles[/b] – homemade 1993 electric vehicles built from car batteries and charged from your Power system
description=[*][b]Dazed Plumbing[/b] – tanks, pipes, pumps, wells, rain collection, purifiers and a water main that brings a whole house back to life
description=[*][b]Dazed Dank[/b] – realistic cannabis growing: strains, grow rooms, hydro, drying, curing and smoking
description=[*][b]Dazed Climate[/b] – a temperature overhaul: real winters, rooms that hold heat, frost on crops and freezing pipes
description=[*][b]Dazed Butchery[/b] – pick a primal from the carcass and trace every cut yourself; hidden grades, field dressing, dry-aging
description=[*][b]Dazed Cooking[/b] – every cut has a best method and a best doneness; cook it right and your survivor feels it
description=[*][b]Dazed Essentials[/b] – common-sense quality of life, every feature a sandbox switch
description=[/list]

description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Core/images/h-authors.png[/img]
description=Dazed Core is a library other mods can build on: a power registry (register a provider or a load and ask [i]isPowered(obj)[/i] about any object), building and reach resolution, synced ModData tables, a client-to-authority request layer with rate limits, mod detection so features can yield to another mod, schema migration, and a shared Error Magnifier report. Full API notes and headless tests are in the GitHub repo.
